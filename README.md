# Disruption Desk

A concept for Emirates: when a flight is disrupted, the passenger hears from the airline first, on WhatsApp, with options they can tap instead of waiting in a phone queue.

**Live demo:** https://shorouk9.github.io/emirates-disruption-desk/

## What the demo shows

Three scenarios, each starting from a lock-screen notification:

- **Missed connection** (London → Dubai → Cairo): seats held in advance, one-tap rebooking, meal voucher, compensation claim opened automatically, live arrival link for family.
- **Cancellation** (Dubai → Mumbai): rebook tomorrow, rebook via a partner, or an automatic refund with a step-by-step tracker.
- **Long delay** (Dubai → Manila): meal vouchers for a family of four, free move to tomorrow, live update for the person picking them up.

Every scenario includes a "Is this really the airline?" check and a handoff to a human agent who already sees the full chat.

## Why

- 27,000 flights were cancelled at Dubai International in the first 30 days of the 2026 conflict (The News, March 2026).
- Transit passengers through Dubai were down 60% in June 2026 while capacity was near 80% (LoyaltyLobby).
- Proactive WhatsApp support was introduced for Gold-tier Skywards members (Emirates Group, 2025-26), while most stranded passengers fly economy.
- Scammers impersonated airline support accounts to target stranded passengers (Euronews, March 2026).

## How it would work

1. **Trigger:** a delay, cancellation or at-risk connection in the ops system sends the first message within minutes.
2. **Approved templates:** business-started messages use Meta-approved WhatsApp templates.
3. **Real actions:** reply buttons and lists connect to rebooking, vouchers and refunds.
4. **Human handoff:** an agent joins with the full trip and chat history.
5. **Anti-scam check:** each template ends with a code passengers can confirm on the official website.

## What to measure

Calls per disrupted passenger · minutes to rebook · days to refund · satisfaction of disrupted passengers · compensation disputes.
