Disruption Desk

When a flight is disrupted, the passenger hears from the airline first, on WhatsApp, with options they can tap instead of a phone queue. Pick a scenario, tap the notification, then reply with the buttons or type your own message.

How it works

  1. Trigger. A delay, cancellation or connection at risk in the ops system sends the first message within minutes.
  2. Approved templates. Business-started messages use Meta-approved templates: delay, cancellation, missed connection, rebooking, voucher and refund.
  3. Real actions. Reply buttons and lists are linked to rebooking, vouchers and refunds, so a tap confirms a seat or issues a voucher.
  4. Human handoff. An agent joins with the full trip and chat history. The passenger never repeats the story.
  5. Anti-scam check. Every template ends with a code passengers can check on the official website.

What to measure

  • Calls per disrupted passenger
  • Minutes to rebook
  • Days to refund
  • Satisfaction of disrupted passengers
  • Compensation disputes

Pilot

Start with Europe → Dubai → South Asia connections during the winter peak. Compare against a control group for eight weeks, then add Arabic, Hindi, Urdu and Tagalog and roll out network-wide.
